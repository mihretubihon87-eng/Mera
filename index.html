Our Programs

Nursing

Bachelor of Nursing program The field of nursing involves an academic field called Nursing,
which trains people to become nurses in hospitals, clinics, and community health services. Nurses have many important responsibilities, such as: Caring for patients (helping with daily needs like feeding, bathing, and comfort) Giving medicines and injections as prescribed by doctors Monitoring patients’ health (checking blood pressure, temperature, etc.) Assisting doctors during treatments and procedures

Pharmacy

Bachelor of Pharmacy program Pharmacy is a healthcare profession that focuses on preparing, dispensing,
and giving advice about medicines to help treat diseases and improve health. pharmacist works in hospitals, clinics, or drug stores and: Dispenses medicines prescribed by doctors Explains how to take medicines safely (dose, time, side effects) Checks if medicines are safe together (drug interactions) Prepares or compounds some medicines

Lab Technology

Medical Laboratory Technology program Medical Laboratory (often called Medical Laboratory Science) is a healthcare
profession that focuses on testing body samples to help detect, diagnose, and
monitor diseases. They usually work in hospitals or labs and: Test blood to check for diseases (like malaria, anemia, infections) Analyze urine and other body fluids Identify bacteria, viruses, and parasites Help doctors diagnose diseases correctly Monitor patient treatment progress Use microscopes and lab machines for accurate results

midewifery

midwifery program Midwifery is a healthcare profession that focuses on caring for pregnant women,
supporting childbirth, and caring for mothers and newborn babies. Midwives work in hospitals, health centers, or maternity clinics and: Care for pregnant women during pregnancy (antenatal care) Check the health of mother and baby Assist during normal delivery (childbirth) Help manage safe labor and reduce complications

Health office

puplic health officer program Public health focuses on preventing disease,
promoting healthy lifestyles, and improving health services for everyone in society. Simple example: Vaccination programs Clean water and sanitation Health education (like hygiene, HIV prevention)
